# NightPdf

NightPdf is a PDF reader for iPhone, iPad, and Mac, made for reading in the dark.

## Support

Questions, bug reports, or feature requests: email **[ziqzhu@ucdavis.edu](mailto:ziqzhu@ucdavis.edu)**.

## Tips

- **Dark pages:** tap ••• → Page Color → Dark (or Match System to follow your device).
- **Knock to Scroll:** tap ••• → Knock to Scroll…, turn it on, and lay your device flat on a table. Knock twice beside it to scroll forward, three times to scroll back. If it misses knocks or scrolls on its own, adjust Knock Strength.
- **Open a PDF:** from the Files app, or with Share → NightPdf in another app.
