# NightPdf Privacy Policy

*Last updated: October 23, 2026*

NightPdf does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

- **No data collection.** The app has no accounts, analytics, advertising, or tracking, and it makes no network requests.
- **Your documents stay on your device.** NightPdf opens PDFs you choose and reads them in place. It never uploads or copies them anywhere.
- **Settings stay on your device.** Your reading preferences and the page you were on in each document are saved only on your device.
- **Motion sensor.** On iPhone and iPad, Knock to Scroll reads the accelerometer while a document is open, only to detect knocks. The readings are processed on the device as they arrive and are never stored or sent anywhere. It's off until you turn it on.

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page.
